**Location:** Charing Cross, Udhagamandalam (Ooty), Tamil Nadu
**Intelligence Status:** Verified & Comprehensive
**Dashboard Classification:** High-Value Retail Asset (Premium Fuel Corridor)

### Key Intelligence Brief
**IndianOil Sakthi Fuel Agencies** represents a critical node in the fuel distribution network of the Nilgiris district, strategically positioned at the high-traffic **Charing Cross junction**. This facility is distinct from standard outlets due to its classification as a **high-performance fuel retailer** (dispensing XP100 and XP95), serving a dual catchment of local premium vehicle owners and the heavy influx of tourist traffic entering Ooty. While the asset boasts strong fundamental economics driven by its unbeatable location and premium product mix, it faces significant operational friction due to severe **traffic congestion** at the ingress/egress points and documented gaps in **amenity compliance** (specifically sanitation facilities). The station operates under a partnership structure (Messrs M. Thirumoorthy & C. Prasathkrishnan) and maintains active regulatory standing with GST and petroleum licensing authorities.
